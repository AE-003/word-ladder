How to play

  • Turn the start word into the target word.
  • Each step, change exactly one letter and submit a real word.
  • Letters that already match the target's letter in that position glow green.
  • Stuck? Use a Hint — it costs a point off your final score but reveals the next best word.
  • Daily gives everyone the same puzzle each day. Random gives a fresh curated puzzle. Custom lets you pick your own start/target words (must be real, connected 4-letter words).
